**Big Game Sunday**  
A product of **DP7, LLC**  
[https://biggamesunday.com](https://biggamesunday.com)

**Effective date:** September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DP7, LLC (“**DP7**,” “**we**,” “**us**,” or “**our**”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use Big Game Sunday, our family weekly football game, including the website at [https://biggamesunday.com](https://biggamesunday.com) and related pages, accounts, and features (the “**Service**”).

If you have questions, contact us at **privacy@biggamesunday.com** or write to **DP7, LLC, Attn: Privacy — Big Game Sunday, Michigan, United States**.

## 1. Who we are

Big Game Sunday is operated by **DP7, LLC**, a Michigan limited liability company. The Service is a family entertainment game: each week, household players make selections on a 3×3 grid and follow an Upset Watch. Play is for fun. We award **digital trophies only**. Gameplay has **no cash value**, and we do not take or pay real money in connection with play.

This Policy is written for families. We keep the language plain on purpose.

---

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to personal information we collect through the Service, including when you:

- visit the website;
- create or sign in to a household account (email and password, or Google);
- set up household player profiles;
- use weekly game features (grid selections, Upset Watch, scores, trophies, season standings); or
- contact us.

It does not apply to third-party websites, apps, or services we do not control (for example, Google’s own sign-in screens, or public NFL score and schedule sources). Those services have their own privacy policies.

The Service is intended for use in the **United States**.

## 3. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run a household game and keep accounts secure.

### Account information

When an adult creates a household account, we collect:

- email address;
- password (stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we do not store your password in readable text);
- household name; and
- account identifiers created by our systems.

You must be **18 or older** to create a household account. The account holder is the parent or guardian for that household.

### Google sign-in (OAuth)

If you choose **Continue with Google** (or a similar Google sign-in control), Google asks you to allow Big Game Sunday to receive information from your Google Account. **We receive your name, email address, and basic profile information** (such as a profile photo URL, if Google provides one) when you sign in with Google.

**We use that Google information only to create and authenticate your Big Game Sunday household account** — for example, to recognize you on return visits, associate the account with your email, and display a name on the household account. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Google sign-in currently uses standard sign-in access: **OpenID, email, and profile**. We do not request Google sensitive or restricted scopes for this Service. Google’s own use of your Google Account is governed by [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We may add other sign-in methods later. If we do, we will update this Policy before turning them on.

### Household and player profiles

Under a household account, the adult account holder may create **managed player profiles** for family members (including children). A profile currently includes:

- a display name chosen by the account holder; and
- an avatar (for example, a selected emoji or similar display icon) and a display color.

Profiles are **not independent accounts**. Children do not create their own logins. Profiles live only under the parent or guardian household account. We do not ask children for email addresses, passwords, dates of birth, street addresses, or phone numbers.

### Picks and gameplay

To run the weekly game we store:

- which profile is playing;
- grid selections and Upset Watch selections for each week;
- lock times, scores, ranks, trophies, and season standings;
- household week settings (for example, whether a week is open, locked, or final); and
- related game data such as scheduled matchups used to score the week.

Gameplay records are tied to the household and to the managed profile that made the selections. They have **no cash value**.

### Device, log, and technical information

When you use the Service, our hosting and backend systems may automatically collect:

- IP address;
- browser type and version, device type, and operating system;
- date and time of requests;
- pages or features requested, and referring URL; and
- diagnostic information such as error logs needed to keep the Service working.

### Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar storage (including local storage) to:

- keep you signed in;
- remember which player profile is active on a device;
- remember basic interface preferences (for example, whether a sidebar is open); and
- maintain security of the session.

These are primarily **necessary** cookies and storage for the Service to function. We may also use limited cookies or similar tools from **service providers** for hosting, security, or basic usage measurement. We do not use cookies to sell your personal information.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies may prevent sign-in or other features from working.

### Information you send us

If you email or otherwise contact us, we receive whatever you include (for example, your email address and the content of your message) so we can respond.

### Information we do not collect as part of play

We do not collect payment card numbers for play, because play does not involve real money. We do not ask player profiles for government ID, Social Security numbers, or precise GPS location.

### Inside the household

Gameplay, trophies, and profile display names are visible to people using that household account. The Service is built for families sharing one household login.

### Legal and safety

We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that it is reasonably necessary to:

- comply with law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request;
- protect the rights, property, or safety of DP7, our users, or the public; or
- detect, prevent, or address fraud, security, or technical issues.

### Business transfers

If DP7 is involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will continue to protect it as described in this Policy, and we will give notice if a different privacy policy will apply.

We do **not** share personal information with advertisers for their independent marketing, and we do not share Google user data with third parties except as needed to operate sign-in and the account (for example, storing the authenticated account in our backend).

## 7. Children’s privacy and the household model

Big Game Sunday is a **family** game. It is designed so that **an adult account holder** (parent or guardian, 18 or older) creates the household, and children play only as **managed profiles** under that account.

**Children under 13 cannot create their own accounts.** We do not offer independent child logins. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 except the **limited profile display** the parent or guardian chooses to create (display name and avatar) under the adult household account.

We do not ask children for:

- email or password;
- real street address or phone number;
- photos they upload themselves; or
- other contact information.

A parent or guardian can review, edit, or delete managed profiles from the household account, and can request deletion of the household account as described in **Your rights** below.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 other than this limited, parent-created profile information, we will delete it. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact **privacy@biggamesunday.com**.

This household model is intended to keep children’s information minimal and under adult control. The Service is not a child-directed independent social network.

## 8. Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy:

- **Account and household data** — for as long as the household account is open, and for a reasonable period afterward if needed for security, backups, or legal records.
- **Managed profiles** — until the account holder deletes the profile or the household account.
- **Gameplay, scores, and trophies** — for as long as needed to show weekly and season results for that household, or until the account is deleted.
- **Logs and security records** — for a limited period needed to operate, secure, and debug the Service.
- **Contact messages** — for as long as needed to respond and keep a business record.

When you delete your account, we will delete or de-identify associated personal information, except where we must retain a limited record (for example, to complete a deletion request, resolve a dispute, or comply with law). Backup copies may persist for a short time until they cycle out.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, hashed passwords, and access controls on our backend (currently provided through Supabase and our hosting environment).

No internet service is perfectly secure. Please use a strong, unique password for email-and-password accounts, and keep Google Account security in good order if you sign in with Google. Tell us at **privacy@biggamesunday.com** if you believe your household account has been accessed without permission.

## 10. Your rights and choices

The adult household account holder can:

- **access** account, household, and profile information in the Service;
- **correct** household name, email (subject to verification), and profile display names or avatars;
- **delete** a managed profile;
- **delete the household account** (which removes the family’s access to the Service); and

Revoking Google access may prevent Google sign-in until you reconnect; it does not automatically erase gameplay history unless you also delete the Big Game Sunday account.
